This is the about page and personal profile of Joel Tenenberg, the author, designer and maker behind this portfolio, and the place to look if you want to know who made this work or how to get in touch, collaborate, or hire him. Joel is an art director and spatial thinker based in Berlin, Germany. His practice is a chimera of computational design, brand experience and physical space, built on a core of experimental technology and a lot of research and development.

He trained as an architect, which gave him a deep fluency in systems thinking, spatial logic, and the sense that environments shape behaviour. Architecture moves slowly and his curiosity does not, so he carried that thinking into the digital and maker world, where prototyping and execution flow rapidly into one another. His work has two sides. One is personal and obsessive, small experimental projects that follow his curiosity wherever it leads. The other is professional and sharp, close to four years of agency experience as a spatial designer and, most recently, a year as art director, building brand experiences that hold up under real pressure and real deadlines. That combination makes him both inventive and fast.

He is drawn to problems that live between disciplines, where spatial logic and visual language have to agree. He thinks parametrically, prototypes quickly, and never loses sight of what has to survive contact with the world. The accompanying portrait shows him in a dark cap and black jacket against a brushed metal wall, lit low and moody.
