Operational Analysis of Photogrammetry is Joel Tenenberg's master's thesis, the largest and most academic work in this portfolio. It is a graduate dissertation and research project completed in 2025 at Universität der Künste Berlin together with TU Berlin, and shown at the UdK Rundgang and the Lange Nacht der Wissenschaften. If you are looking for the thesis, the master project, or the academic, theoretical and written research work, this is it.

The research opens the black box of photogrammetry, the reconstruction of three dimensional space from overlapping photographs that now sits invisibly inside Google Maps, autonomous navigation and biometric surveillance. Rather than treating it as a neutral, objective tool, the thesis follows each algorithmic step, feature detection, image matching, pose estimation and point cloud construction, as an interdependent chain where early choices cascade through the whole pipeline, and argues that these operative moments encode hidden values and assumptions. It draws on Operational Analysis by Friedrich and Hoel and Simondon's theory of transduction, working with structure from motion, SLAM and ORB-SLAM3.

The work has two outputs, a large printed operational map of the entire algorithm pipeline, and an interactive real time installation built entirely in TouchDesigner with Python and Metashape. Visually it reads as a laboratory instrument, a tall silver aluminium truss tower carrying several monitors, showing a neon green interface on black with live numerical readouts, dials and cascading parameters, and a live camera feed overlaid with bright green keypoint circles marking detected features. Technical line drawings show the tower and its mounted screens.

Themes a visitor might search for include master thesis, dissertation, graduate research, computer vision, point cloud, 3D reconstruction, algorithmic opacity, surveillance, mapping, media theory, software studies, and critical technology.
