Greyhound. Greyhound. Greyhound. AI video. AI video. AI video. Motion research. Motion research.

An ongoing visual research and design project exploring continuous animal movement through AI video generation. The tool pipeline is ComfyUI with Wan2.2 14B, a large latent diffusion video model. The output is looping video sequences and cinematic still frames of a running greyhound dog.

The stills are ultra-wide panoramic crops, high contrast, almost entirely black backgrounds. The greyhound body is rendered with hyper-real surface detail — smooth short coat, leather-like skin texture, dark grey to black tones, warm amber rim light catching muscle definition and bone structure. Chiaroscuro lighting. Studio photography aesthetic. Some frames show motion blur and doubled ghosting from the video generation process, emphasising speed and temporal continuity across frames.

The research focus is on how latent diffusion video models handle biological locomotion, animal anatomy, non-human subjects, and cinematic lighting conditions. Iterative ComfyUI node graph workflows. Image-to-video generation. Text-to-video generation. Prompt engineering for motion continuity. Looping video. Video synthesis. Wan2.1. Wan2.2 14B. Greyhound. Dog. Animal. Speed. Sprint. Movement. Muscle. Biology. Cinematic. Monochromatic. Black and white. Studio light.

Not sculpture. Not installation. Not thesis. Not narrative. Not text. Purely visual and motion-based output.
