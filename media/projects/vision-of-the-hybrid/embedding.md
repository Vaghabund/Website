Vision of the Hybrid is an artistic project from 2024, made at UdK Berlin and shown in the Re/Embodied Data exhibition and the UdK Rundgang. It is a work of speculative and critical design, an artwork rather than a tool or a thesis. At its centre is a sculptural object, a spiky, organic, biomorphic form somewhere between a sea urchin, a seed pod and an abstract body, covered in tapering spikes. It appears in two guises, a physical 3D print that people can hold and touch, and a rendered version with a deep glossy black surface catching cool metallic silver highlights, floating on a pale grey background. It is presented on white poster sheets that carry small technical metadata captions, and one photograph shows a print of it held up against a blue sky beside a concrete building.

The object is designed to provoke rather than instruct, inviting tactile, haptic, embodied exploration. Around it runs a non-linear narrative of seven short diary-like fragments, written together with artificial intelligence using Whisper voice transcription, an intimate first person voice describing a strange humming companion object, a phasma or wandering stranger, and a longing to connect with the other. The work draws on Rosi Braidotti's posthuman and feminist thought, on relational thinking, hybridity, metamorphosis, identity, gender and otherness, imagining futures beyond the current status quo. It was modelled and rendered with Blender, Cinema 4D and NomadSculpt, with the text shaped through Whisper.

A visitor might reach it searching for sculpture, 3D printed object, spiky black form, posthumanism, feminist theory, speculative design, AI co-writing and co-authorship, diary narrative, worldbuilding, or the politics of design.
