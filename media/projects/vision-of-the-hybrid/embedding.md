Vision of the Hybrid. Artistic project, 2024, Politics of Design, UdK Berlin. Speculative design, embodied experience, generative narrative, posthumanism, AI co-authorship, worldbuilding.

A speculative design project centered on a 3D-printed sculptural object and a narrative co-authored with artificial intelligence. The work interrogates identity, gender, class, race, and technology as contested categories. Drawing on Rosi Braidotti's relational thinking, the human condition is not fixed but continually reshaped by technologies we inhabit.

A 3D-printed physical object provokes rather than instructs — visitors engage tactilely, manipulating the form to access meaning. The generative narrative, co-authored with AI via Whisper, is non-linear and fragmented. Seven diary-like entries narrate an ambiguous human-object relationship. The object dissolves boundaries between self and other, human and technology, creating space for questions about otherness, hybridity, and posthuman futures.

The project imagines worlds beyond current structures of power and technological determinism. Tools: Blender, Cinema4D, NomadSculpt, Aftereffects, Whisper. Keywords: speculative design, embodied interaction, generative narrative, AI writing, 3D printing, sculpture, posthuman, identity, technology, relational thinking, Braidotti, feminist theory, worldbuilding, otherness, hybrid, future, nonbinary, interactive design.
