Operational Analysis of Photogrammetry

Master Thesis · 2025

Overview

An exploration of how photogrammetry can be operationalized for rapid site capture. The project combines iterative image pipelines, point cloud validation, and lightweight 3D visualization to translate raw captures into usable spatial intelligence.

Challenge

Capturing consistent geometry across varying light conditions and lens distortions while keeping processing fast enough for on-site decisions.

Solution

Built a modular workflow: calibrated capture presets, feature matching with outlier rejection, and quick-look renders to flag gaps before leaving the field. A companion UI lets collaborators review frames, point clouds, and reconstruction steps in sequence.

Role

Research, pipeline design, visualization
