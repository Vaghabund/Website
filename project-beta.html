Project Beta

UI/UX Design · 2024

Overview

A banking app redesign focused on accessibility and confident money management. The work spanned research, interaction models, and a component library tuned for both mobile and tablet breakpoints.

Challenge

Legacy flows were dense and color contrast failed WCAG. Navigation varied across platforms, confusing returning customers.

Solution

Built a unified IA, simplified transfer flows to two taps, and introduced responsive typography tokens with guaranteed contrast. Prototypes were validated in remote testing before handoff.

Role

UX designer & researcher

Timeline

6 months

Technologies

  • Figma
  • React Native
  • Accessibility testing
  • Design systems
