Project Delta

Brand Identity · 2023

Overview

Identity for a sustainable fashion label. The system pairs confident typography with restrained earth tones, covering logo, packaging, and digital storefront.

Challenge

Communicate premium quality without drifting away from the brand’s ecological commitments.

Solution

Developed a modular wordmark, recyclable packaging specs, and a digital style guide that keeps materials, color, and messaging consistent across campaigns.

Role

Brand designer & art director

Timeline

3 months

Technologies

  • Adobe Creative Suite
  • Figma
  • Print production
